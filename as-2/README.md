### **Feedback:**
I enjoyed this assignment. I spent about 6 to 8 hours on it. At least half of that time was dedicated to either playing with the shape settings (user testing) or trying to get my bismuth crystal custom shape to look just how I wanted it to. I was very intimidated when I unpacked the tar file; it was a lot of files, and the 81-page PDF did not help my confidence. But, after sifting through the project files and PDF, I realized the scope of this assignment really wasn't too big. Overall, I would say this assignment was easier than assignment 1.

A small part of me wishes I could have spent time writing the GUI code because ImGui seems like a neat library to learn, but still, I appreciate not having to deal with all the boilerplate setup. For my custom shape, I really wanted to implement some kind of fractal, but after a little research, I realized that fractals are inter-dimensional shapes that in some way are a rebellion against calculus because they conflict with the idea that if you zoom in enough on a curve it becomes smooth. So, I realized fractals are a lot cooler than I thought, and I also realized that they are fundamentally really difficult to implement with procedural polygon generation. They are better to implement in shaders. Regardless, for my custom shape, I ended up going with a bismuth crystal, which is an equally fascinating formation. 

If I could do anything to help other students on this assignment, I would tell them to try to understand the basic hierarchy of the project before starting anything. Investigating the main, application, renderer, and shape files provides useful insight and makes you realize that the structure isn't that complicated. Thank you for the assignment!
